On September 10, 2025, at 12:23 in the afternoon, Charlie Kirk was answering a student's question in a courtyard at Utah Valley University when a single shot killed him in front of roughly three thousand people and a half-dozen cameras. The State of Utah has charged Tyler Robinson, then twenty-two, with aggravated murder and says it will seek the death penalty. He has not entered a plea, no jury has heard the case, and he is presumed innocent — a sentence that belongs in the second paragraph of this article and not the footnotes, because everything else on this page must be read through it.

{plate:IMG-KRK-P01}

This case does not ask who killed Charlie Kirk. That is a question for a courtroom, and no claim on this page grades it. What this case maps is the *record*: what the public evidence actually contains, where it is strong, where it stops, and which of the disputes swirling around it are real, manufactured, or simply unresolved. The killing of a political figure, on camera, during an active culture war, has produced an evidentiary argument conducted almost entirely in summaries of summaries. The method here is the opposite: every consequential sentence below is pinned to a claim you can open, every claim to evidence, every record to a source that was independently verified before it entered this file.

## What is actually established

Begin with what no serious party disputes. [A person was on the Losee Center roof and fled across it within seconds of the shot]{claim=KRK-C001} — captured in social-media video independently analyzed by the Washington Post's visual-forensics team, in footage the FBI released of the figure jumping from the roof, and in the sworn testimony of the first officer up there, who found a red-and-black screwdriver and a disturbance in the gravel "like someone had lain there in the prone position." [A towel-wrapped .30-06 bolt-action rifle was recovered in a wooded area along the apparent escape route]{claim=KRK-C002}, where the FBI says the fleeing figure went.

The medical record, as far as it goes, is equally direct: [the medical examiner's report classifies the death as a homicide caused by a gunshot wound to the neck, with projectile fragments recovered at autopsy]{claim=KRK-C006}. And the prosecution's association case is substantial: [DNA evidence presented at the preliminary hearing links Robinson to the rifle and related items]{claim=KRK-C008}, with statistics the state's witnesses described as "extremely likely," and [testimony and exhibits attribute incriminating statements to him]{claim=KRK-C009} — a roommate's recorded account, extracted texts, a Discord message before surrender. Anyone telling you this is a house of cards has not read the record.

## Where the record stops

Anyone telling you the record is closed has not read it either. The strongest possible ballistic proposition — *this fatal projectile came from this rifle* — is precisely the one the public record does not contain. [No public, conclusive identification of the autopsy fragments to the recovered rifle exists]{claim=KRK-C003}: [the ATF examiner's comparison was inconclusive, neither identifying nor excluding the rifle]{claim=KRK-C004}. Both sides of the public argument have distorted that single result — "the bullet matched" is false, and so is "the rifle was cleared." When a defense filing surfaced the inconclusive result in March 2026, the ensuing media wave was fought between exactly those two misreadings.

Worse, the fragments themselves do not yet reconcile. [Photographs documented seven recovered fragments; the examiner received four; and no public accounting explains the difference]{claim=KRK-C005}. There may be an entirely routine explanation — fragments retained by the medical examiner, consumed in testing, separately packaged. None has been published. That is a genuine chain-of-custody question, and this case ranks its resolution as the single highest-value disclosure available.

The same pattern holds for the medical evidence: the classification is in the record, but [the complete autopsy documentation that would let independent pathologists test competing mechanism claims is not publicly available]{claim=KRK-C007} — the report entered through an investigator, over a defense objection that it was "an expert report without an expert present." And the DNA, powerful as it is for *whose* biological material, does not by itself answer *when or how it got there*; the state's own forensic biologist acknowledged the transfer scenario as possible under cross-examination.

Add it together and you reach this case's central finding so far: [the public record does not yet contain what an independent auditor would need to verify the official account end to end]{claim=KRK-C019}. That sentence must be handled with precision, because two different audiences want to round it in two different directions. It does not mean the official account is false — every enumerated gap has mundane explanations, and pre-trial records are always incomplete. It means *not fully auditable*, and the honest response to "not fully auditable" is disclosure and testing, not a verdict in either direction.

## The custody file

The event's own cameras are a study in how provenance problems are born. Sworn testimony establishes that the event videographer supplied investigators with video the same day — a flash drive, then downloadable files. But those are copies, and [whether every original storage card reached law-enforcement custody is not publicly established]{claim=KRK-C012}; the defense has already told the court that "someone made clips, and we don't know who." Meanwhile, [footage published by Candace Owens appears to show the contractor removing the rear camera's card within about four minutes of the shot]{claim=KRK-C011} — an act whose occurrence looks documented and whose purpose is not. Grabbing your own equipment in a catastrophe is what a working videographer does; it is also what someone controlling an angle would do. The footage cannot tell you which, and neither can this site. What would: the media ledger this case's research agenda specifies — serials, hashes, acquisition logs.

The physical scene has its own version. [The courtyard was excavated and paved within days, and the lead investigator testified he did not personally authorize it]{claim=KRK-C013} — documented in records obtained under Utah's open-records law, down to a $6,090.52 emergency invoice reading "Clean up for shooting incident." Whether that mattered forensically depends entirely on which mechanism hypothesis you hold, which is why this case records the paving as a real transparency failure while declining the leap to [the claim that documented institutional conduct establishes deliberate evidence concealment]{claim=KRK-C017} — a leap the current record does not license.

## The pressure file

Some of the most consequential material has nothing to do with forensics. [In messages authenticated by TPUSA's own spokesman, Kirk described losing a two-million-dollar-a-year donor over his refusal to cancel Tucker Carlson, and wrote that donor pressure was leaving him "no choice but to leave the pro-Israel cause"]{claim=KRK-C014}. That is an extraordinary window into his final weeks, and it is real — confirmed on the record, not alleged. Reporting of a contentious Hamptons gathering the month before adds a disputed chapter: anonymous sources describe an "intervention"; the named participants categorically deny coercion.

Here the discipline of this site does its heaviest lifting. A motive environment is not an attribution. [Nothing in the public record connects any donor, organization, or state to the killing operationally]{claim=KRK-C015} — no communications, no payments, no logistics, no forensic link; even the publication that broke the authenticated texts states that plainly. The pressure was real. The bridge does not exist in public evidence. Both halves of that sentence are findings, and this site will keep displaying them together.

## The alternative mechanism

The most technically ambitious challenge to the official account holds that [a device at Kirk's microphone, not a distant rifle shot, caused the fatal injury]{claim=KRK-C018}. Its proponents point to frame-by-frame video interpretation — shirt deformation, a violently displaced necklace, an apparent gas puff before mechanical movement — and one anonymous analyst has published energy estimates, code, and source video inviting anyone to re-run the computation. Against it stands nearly everything else in the record: the gunshot classification, the recovered fragments, the rooftop chain, and the absence of any published residue or device-fragment finding. Even the theory's originator has lately entertained a variant in which a bullet did arrive — at the microphone.

This site grades the hypothesis weakly supported and does not expect that grade to be the last word, because the theory is unusually *testable*: a blind, native-file synchronization study ordering shot, deformation, and wound frame-accurately; laboratory examination of the microphone hardware and clothing; the full autopsy package. Every one of those tests appears in the research agenda. A theory that stakes itself on checkable physics deserves the check — that is the difference between examining an idea and either laundering or burying it.

## Institutions, narratives, and the timeline wars

Officials have not earned unlimited deference here, and that is now a matter of record rather than suspicion: [a judge held a deputy prosecutor in civil contempt in June 2026 for violating pretrial-publicity restrictions]{claim=KRK-C016} after he told a tabloid the state had "ample evidence" of guilt — even as the same ruling found his correction of ballistics misreporting legitimate. Narrative management inside the prosecution was real. So was actual misinformation for it to manage. Both facts are in the file.

The popular counter-narratives fare no better under inspection. The widely circulated timeline case — an unauthenticated 8:07 a.m. video supposedly at odds with an 8:29 arrival, a rooftop flight supposedly too fast for rifle disassembly — collapses on contact with its own premises: [the anomalies are not, on current evidence, inconsistent with the official chronology]{claim=KRK-C010}, and the disassembly claim appears nowhere in the reviewed official record. The related charge that [the FBI director's announced screwdriver-DNA evidence was quietly abandoned]{claim=KRK-C032} splits cleanly under verification: the announcement-versus-charging-document mismatch is real, and the abandonment is false — the evidence was presented under oath ten months later. This case keeps such material where it belongs: as leads in the catalog — the venue-selection allegation, the disputed accounts of who ordered cameras removed, an assertion that the FBI holds the original cards, a lead about a commentator's whereabouts that its own source says is unconfirmed — each anchored to its exact quote, each awaiting the specific verification it names, none promoted to fact.

## The organized-operation file

The strongest compilation of the broader-plot case circulating publicly assembles a procurement record, a flight, and a disaster into a single arc — and it is exactly the material this site's method exists to take apart carefully, because parts of it are real. The federal contract is real: [award N0016425PJ538, "miniaturized-XS demolition charges, anti personnel-XS," $440,494, delivered by August 25, 2025, is verbatim in the federal procurement record]{claim=KRK-C025}, and a live search replicates the claim that it is the only such-worded award in the database. The disaster is real: the manufacturer's Tennessee facility exploded on October 10, 2025, killing sixteen. What verification does not find is the connective tissue — the flight from Heber City has no source beyond assertion, the federal investigation attributes the day's production to commercial cast boosters with the cause formally open, state inspectors documented safety failures severe enough for their largest-ever penalty, and archive forensics shows the device theory was public on September 20, not "two days before" the blast, deflating the reactive-destruction reading while confirming the humbler fact that the theory came first. Real record, real catastrophe, no established bridge — and a research entry (KRK-R012) that names the exact papers that would build or demolish one.

The same discipline sorts the aftermath allegations into three piles. Documented: [surrender-related surveillance footage was allowed to auto-delete unpreserved after thirty days]{claim=KRK-C026}, in the sheriff's office's own records responses; and [the former counterterrorism-center director states on the record that his foreign-nexus inquiry was halted, attributing the order to the FBI director]{claim=KRK-C027} — an assertion by its source, with the FBI's stated rationale (witness exposure at trial) carried alongside it. Open: [whether a bystander's instant false confession was pre-arranged]{claim=KRK-C028} — his diversion *intent* is court record in his own words; any arrangement behind it is, so far, evidenced by nothing. Corrected: the "sealed black box" framing fails on inspection — cameras have repeatedly been *allowed* over defense objection, in-person appearances have occurred, and much of the sealing is defense-driven; what is real is the publicity order a prosecutor was held in contempt under, already graded above.

At the bottom of the ladder sit the leads that currently rest on nothing checkable — an aircraft whose passenger claims have no source at all, a device count from a pseudonymous account citing an anonymous tipster, a secondhand report of Kirk's private fear — kept in the catalog with their provenance stated plainly, because the honest way to handle a lead is neither to publish it as fact nor to pretend it was never raised.

## What would settle it

A preliminary-hearing judge will hear probable-cause arguments on September 1, 2026, and the record will move. But the disputes this case tracks will be settled by disclosures and tests, not rulings: reconcile the seven fragments; release the autopsy package to independent pathologists; publish the media ledger with hashes; run the blind synchronization study; re-examine the ballistics blind with modern metrology; evaluate the DNA at the activity level; drive the 8:07 route with a stopwatch. Every one is specified in the research agenda with what it would prove and disprove. The honest summary of this case today fits in two sentences. The state has assembled a substantial, multi-channel account that no public evidence refutes. The public record is not yet complete enough for anyone — including the state's defenders — to audit that account independently, and until it is, this page will say exactly which pieces are missing.
