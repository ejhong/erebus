Executive summary
The September 11, 2001 attacks killed 2,977 victims — 2,753 at the World Trade Center, 184 at the
Pentagon, and 40 in Pennsylvania — and have since killed thousands more through exposure-related
illness: the CDC’s World Trade Center Health Program counted 132,530 enrolled responders and
survivors and 6,897 deceased members (all causes) as of March 2024. (Britannica) The oﬀicial
account — nineteen al-Qaeda hijackers, four commandeered airliners, impact-plus-fire collapses in New
York — rests on an enormous, convergent record: flight data recorders, DNA identification of victims
including the hijackers’ remains by elimination, seismic and video timing, thousands of interviews, and
the largest building-failure investigation ever conducted, NIST’s 47-report, ~11,000-page WTC study.
(wikipedia.org) Osama bin Laden publicly claimed responsibility in a 2004 video, and Khalid Sheikh
Mohammed told a Guantánamo tribunal in 2007: “I was responsible for the 9/11 operation, from A to
Z.” (wikipedia.org)

And yet the case is not closed in the way most people assume — not because the demolition theories
are right, but because specific, checkable fractures remain in the oﬀicial record, and some of them are
load-bearing for public trust. The honest state of play, decomposed:

  • Established without serious dispute: the four flights, their hijackers, the timeline, al-Qaeda’s
     operational responsibility, the impact-plus-fire initiation of the Twin Towers’ collapses, the presence
     of anomalous pre-attack option trading, and the fact that U.S. agencies held fragmented pre-attack
     warnings — “the system was blinking red.” (Human Factors 101)
  • Documented oﬀicial misconduct: the EPA’s “air is safe” statements were shaped by White
     House edits and made without suﬀicient data (EPA Inspector General, 2003); FAA and NORAD
     gave the 9/11 Commission inaccurate testimony about the air-defense response; the CIA withheld
     and destroyed interrogation videotapes the Commission had sought. (wikipedia.org)
  • Genuinely contested in the technical literature: the cause of WTC 7’s collapse, where NIST’s
     “fire-induced progressive collapse” model (2008) faces the University of Alaska Fairbanks’ four-year
     reevaluation (2020) concluding “fire did not cause the collapse of WTC 7” — while NIST
     has withheld 68,500 of ~91,114 modeling data files under a “might jeopardize public safety” finding
     upheld in court. (NIST)
  • Unresolved and active in court: the extent of Saudi state-linked support for the hijackers.

     the families’ case to trial. (wikipedia.org)

1. How to read this dossier
Erebus’s method is to decompose a controversial hypothesis into atomic claims, grade each on two
axes — credibility (is the local claim true?) and diagnosticity (how much does it favor one hypothesis
over its rivals?) — map evidence records for and against with explicit direction, and keep what the source
says strictly separate from what we infer from it. This dossier follows that discipline throughout. Where
the two sides of a dispute agree on a fact (the 2.25 seconds of free fall; the 4,744 UAL puts), the fact
is marked established, and the contest is relocated to the interpretation, which is where it actually
lives. This single move dissolves roughly half of the popular 9/11 debate, which mostly argues about
observations both parties already accept.

The dossier also adopts Erebus’s argument ladder: observation claims (what was measured) sit at the
bottom, mechanism claims (how it physically worked) in the middle, attribution claims (who is responsible)
at the top. The pattern in the 9/11 record is striking and is the spine of this report: the observations
are largely settled and often genuinely anomalous; the mechanisms are contested in a few specific places;
and the attributions — “inside job,” “controlled demolition,” “foreknowledge” — are the least supported
rung, surviving mainly on the (real) documented instances of oﬀicial deception elsewhere in the record.
Readers should also note what this report deliberately does not do: it does not adjudicate motives, and it
treats the victims’ families — who drove the creation of the 9/11 Commission, the declassification of the
28 pages, the EPA reckoning, and the Saudi litigation — as the central stakeholders of every unresolved
question, not as props for either side.

2. The verified skeleton: what happened, and the toll
2.1 The timeline both sides must accept

Before any contested claim can be evaluated, the uncontested chronological skeleton must be fixed, be-
cause several popular claims die on contact with it. The timing below comes from NIST’s video analysis
(impact and collapse times calibrated to broadcast time stamps), the NTSB’s flight path study for Amer-
ican 77, and Columbia’s Lamont-Doherty seismographs, which independently fixed the tower impacts at
8:46:26 and 9:02:54 within seconds of NIST’s 8:46:30 and 9:02:59. (Human Factors 101) American Air-
lines Flight 11 struck the North Tower at 8:46:30 a.m.; United 175 struck the South Tower at 9:02:59;
American 77 hit the Pentagon at 9:37:45 at roughly 460 knots; the South Tower collapsed at 9:58:59 —
56 minutes after impact; United 93 crashed near Shanksville, Pennsylvania at 10:03:11; the North Tower
collapsed at 10:28:22 — 102 minutes after impact; and WTC 7, never struck by an aircraft, collapsed
at 5:20:52 p.m. after burning for almost seven hours. (Human Factors 101)

2.2 The toll, immediate and deferred

The immediate count is precise and brutal: 2,977 victims excluding the 19 hijackers — 2,753 at the
World Trade Center (including 343 FDNY firefighters, 71 law-enforcement oﬀicers, and 8 private
EMTs), 184 at the Pentagon (125 in the building, 59 aboard Flight 77), and 40 aboard Flight 93; citizens
of at least 102 countries died. (wikipedia.org) NIST’s own tower accounting — 2,735 deaths in the towers
including those aboard the two aircraft — gives the finer breakdown: everyone above the 91st floor of
WTC 1 died; in WTC 2, the 18-minute gap between impacts allowed most below-impact occupants to
escape, and roughly 40% of occupants had already left before the second strike. (NIST)

The deferred toll is the part that changes the case’s moral weight a quarter-century on. The collapse

3. The oﬀicial investigations — and their own documented limits
3.1 The investigative architecture

Four federal or professional efforts anchor the conventional record. The Joint Inquiry of the House and
Senate intelligence committees (final report December 2002, public July 2003) mapped the intelligence
failures and concluded that while significant al-Qaeda threat reporting existed, “none of it identified the
time, place, and specific nature of the attacks.” (Levin Center for Legislative Oversight and Democracy)
The 9/11 Commission (report July 22, 2004) produced the canonical narrative and 41 recommendations,
while itself documenting the insider-trading trace-outs and the watchlisting failures. (History) On the
engineering side, FEMA’s Building Performance Study (FEMA 403, May 2002) was the preliminary
federal look, superseded by the NIST investigation mandated by the National Construction Safety
Team Act: the towers’ final report (NCSTAR 1, October 2005, with 42 companion volumes) and the WTC
7 report (NCSTAR 1A, November 2008). (wtc7.net) The ASCE Pentagon Building Performance
Report (January 2003) covered Arlington. (ASCE)

NIST’s effort was historically large: roughly 200 technical experts, ~7,000 video segments, ~7,000 pho-
tographs, interviews with over 1,000 people, metallurgical and mechanical analysis of 236 recovered
steel pieces, and end-to-end simulations that took about two months per tower and eight months for
WTC 7 on then-current hardware. (U.S. Department of Commerce) Its conclusions — impact damage
plus dislodged fireproofing plus multi-floor fires for the towers; thermal expansion, girder walk-off, and
Column 79 buckling for WTC 7 — are treated claim-by-claim below. The investigation also produced 30
recommendations for the towers and 13 for WTC 7, and NIST credits them with more than 40 changes
to building and fire codes — an important datum in itself, because it shows the oﬀicial investigation found
the existing built environment genuinely vulnerable to what happened, which cuts against any claim that
the collapses required exotic causes to be worth learning from. (U.S. Department of Commerce)

3.2 The Commission’s self-documented weaknesses

Any dossier that treats the 9/11 Commission Report as scripture fails Erebus’s provenance standard,
because the Commission’s own principals documented its defects. Co-chairs Thomas Kean and Lee
Hamilton titled their 2006 memoir Without Precedent and concluded the panel was “set up to fail”:
created late, given $3 million initially against its needs, opposed by the White House for over a year,
and — in Hamilton’s words — deceived by key agencies including NORAD and the FAA. (wikipedia.org)
The Commission was forced to use subpoenas to obtain FAA and NORAD material; staff director John
Farmer said he was “shocked at how different the truth was from the way it was described,” after tapes
showed NORAD had never had any hijacked airliner in its sights and at one point chased the long-crashed
American 11 as a phantom. (wikipedia.org)

Two further provenance caveats attach to the report’s central chapters. First, NBC News’s 2008 anal-
ysis found that more than a quarter of the report’s footnotes trace to CIA interrogations of
detainees subjected to “enhanced interrogation techniques” — later determined to be torture — and
executive director Philip Zelikow conceded that “quite a bit, if not most” of the plot narrative came
from interrogations; at least four cited detainees said they told interrogators what would stop the torture.
(wikipedia.org) Second, the CIA in November 2005 destroyed videotapes of those interrogations
despite the Commission’s 2003–04 requests for interrogation material, prompting Kean and Hamilton to
accuse the agency of consciously impeding the inquiry; the CIA countered that staff never specifically
asked for videos. (wikipedia.org) Philip Shenon’s The Commission (2008) separately documented Ze-
likow’s undisclosed White House contacts during the inquiry. (wikipedia.org) None of this falsifies the
report’s core findings — but it establishes, in Erebus terms, that the canonical narrative’s sourcing is
weaker at exactly the nodes (plot origin, detainee testimony, air-defense timeline) where independent
corroboration is thinnest, and it is legitimate to grade those nodes accordingly.

3.3 NIST’s self-imposed limits

NIST’s engineering reports carry their own documented scope limits, which both sides routinely over-
read in opposite directions. The towers’ investigation explicitly covered events from aircraft impact
to collapse initiation; NIST’s own FAQ states that once collapse began, “the propagation of the collapse
was readily explained without the same complexity of modeling,” and its report’s footnote concedes the
“probable collapse sequence” contains “little analysis of the structural behavior of the tower after the
conditions for collapse initiation were reached.” (NIST) NIST did not test recovered steel for explosive or
thermite residues, judging the fire/impact evidence conclusive and residue analysis non-diagnostic (sulfur
being ubiquitous in gypsum wallboard). (NIST) For WTC 7, no identified steel was recovered —
unmarked pieces had been recycled before they could be catalogued, unlike the color-coded tower steel —
a fact NIST states openly and which remains the single largest physical-evidence gap in the whole case.
(NIST)

The sharpest transparency limit is legal, not technical. Under §7(d) of the NCST Act, the NIST Director
found in July 2009 that releasing parts of the WTC 7 modeling data “might jeopardize public safety,”
and NIST ultimately withheld 68,500 of 91,114 responsive data files while releasing 25,644; the
U.S. District Court for D.C. upheld the withholding in Quick v. NIST (April 7, 2011), on the ground
that detailed connection models validated against a real collapse could instruct someone how to destroy
similar buildings. (GovInfo) Whatever its legal soundness, the practical consequence stands: the initiating
mechanism of the most contested building collapse in history cannot be fully reproduced by independent
engineers from NIST’s own published materials, thirteen years after the report — and the University of
Alaska study discussed in §5 exists precisely in that vacuum. (Architects & Engineers for 9/11 Truth)

5. WTC 7 — the case’s true crux
5.1 Why a 47-story building nobody was killed in carries the case

WTC 7 killed no one — FDNY abandoned firefighting roughly three hours before the collapse, and
the ~4,000 occupants evacuated — yet it is the load-bearing node of the entire 9/11 controversy, for a
structural reason: it removes the two confounders that explain the towers. No aircraft hit it, and
no jet fuel soaked it. If oﬀice fires alone, ignited by debris, brought down a modern 47-story steel-frame
building in a few hours, that is (a) the first such event in recorded engineering history — NIST itself
called it “the first known instance of fire causing the total collapse of a tall building” — and
(b) an event whose mechanics must be airtight, because the whole demolition reading of 9/11 stands or
falls here. (NIST) Debris from WTC 1, 370 feet away, ignited fires on at least ten floors; only the fires on



                                                                                        Generated by Kimi
floors 7–9 and 11–13 burned out of control, fed by ordinary oﬀice contents, after the towers’ collapse
cut the city water main feeding the lower-floor sprinklers. (NIST)

NIST’s probable collapse sequence, finalized in NCSTAR 1A (November 2008), runs: thermal expansion
of long-span floor beams on the east side → the Floor-13 girder between Columns 79 and 44 (A2001)
pushed off its seat → Floor 13 collapses → cascading floor failures leave Column 79 unsupported over
nine stories → Column 79 buckles (the initiation event, visible as the east penthouse sinking) → failure
progresses upward and then east-to-west through the core (columns 58–78) → the façade buckles between
floors 7 and 17 and the building falls as a unit. (NIST) Critically, NIST’s own thermal analysis found
column temperatures stayed below ~300 °C and only east-side floor beams exceeded 600 °C — the
mechanism runs on thermal expansion, not steel “melting,” at temperatures hundreds of degrees below
conventional fire-resistance assumptions. (NIST) This is a scientifically respectable, internally consistent
account. It is also, uniquely among the day’s events, an account for which no identified physical steel
from the building survives in evidence. (NIST)

5.2 The free fall: the fact, and the fight over what it means

The case’s most famous number is now agreed by everyone. In the August 2008 draft report, NIST stated
the north face descended 18 stories in 5.4 seconds — 40% longer than free fall — and lead investigator
Shyam Sunder, challenged by physics teacher David Chandler at the public briefing, answered: “A free-
fall time would be an object that has no structural components below it.” (International Fire & Safety
Journal) After the comment period, the final report re-analyzed the roofline motion and concluded the
descent had three stages: Stage 1 (0–1.75 s) slower than gravity as exterior columns buckled; Stage 2
(1.75–4.0 s) at gravitational acceleration — free fall — for approximately 2.25 seconds over
~32 m (105 ft, ~8 stories), during which “the buckled columns provided negligible support to the
upper portion”; Stage 3 (4.0–5.4 s) decelerating as the falling face met debris resistance. (NIST)

Both readings of this fact are internally coherent, and the dossier keeps them separate. NIST’s: once the
interior had progressively collapsed (the east penthouse fell ~4 seconds before the exterior moved), the
shell had essentially nothing inside it for those eight stories; free fall of the hollowed façade is the expected

signature of the fire-progressive sequence, not a refutation of it — and NIST’s structural model showed
buckled columns losing load capacity at exactly that interval. (NIST) The challengers’: 2.25 seconds
of symmetric, building-wide free fall requires every one of the ~81 columns across eight stories to lose
nearly all resistance simultaneously, an event fire — a spatially and temporally diffuse process — does
not obviously produce; Chandler’s independent tracking showed all visible roofline points transitioning to

is real but bounded — free fall is a necessary feature of any plausible account, NIST’s included, so it
discriminates less than either side advertises. What genuinely discriminates is the initiation — which is
where the UAF study aimed.

5.3 The UAF reevaluation (2020): the strongest challenge on the record

Between 2015 and 2019, a University of Alaska Fairbanks team led by forensic structural engineer J.
Leroy Hulsey — funded with $316,153 from Architects & Engineers for 9/11 Truth — rebuilt
WTC 7 in SAP2000 and ABAQUS from the structural drawings and re-ran the fire response. Its March
25, 2020 final report, A Structural Reevaluation of the Collapse of World Trade Center 7, concluded: “fire
did not cause the collapse of WTC 7” — fires could not have produced the member displacements
NIST’s initiation requires (the girder could not walk off past Column 79’s side plate; the east-side framing
was stiffer than modeled) — and, secondarily, that the observed behavior is matched only by near-
simultaneous failure of every column in the building (core columns over eight stories, then exterior
columns 1.3 s later). (INE University of Alaska Fairbanks) The team released its entire dataset — 256
GB compressed — for public replication, a transparency standard NIST has not met. (INE University
of Alaska Fairbanks)

The counter-case is equally specific. NIST, denying AE911Truth’s 2020 Request for Correction, an-
swered the girder objection directly: the 16-story ANSYS model’s connection detail is consistent with the
fabrication shop drawings, with the A2001 bolts on a seated connection attached to the exterior edges of
Column 79’s side plates — i.e., NIST claims the side plate was modeled, and the request was denied on
that basis. (metabunk.org) Independent engineering critics (the Metabunk analysis community being the
most thorough public record) found category-level flaws in the UAF work: it modeled a single static
temperature distribution rather than migrating fires; it focused on one connection when NIST’s ini-
tiation involved several; its displacement comparisons mixed global and local frames; and its Section 4.6
“best fit” simulation reproduces only the trivial feature — free fall, which any column-removal scenario
yields by construction — while failing the observed kink in the roofline, the east-penthouse se-
quence timing, and the rotational deformation seen in video. (metabunk.org) Two reviews were
solicited from academics sympathetic to the sponsor (e.g., McMaster emeritus Robert Korol), not from
the fire-engineering mainstream; the paper has not passed independent journal peer review. (Construct-
Connect)

The dossier’s honest grade: NYC-C012 (NIST’s fire mechanism) is “mixed” — internally
consistent, produced by the best-resourced investigation, but never independently reproduced and
resting on withheld model files; NYC-C013 (UAF’s simultaneous-failure requirement) is
“weakly_supported” — a serious modeling effort with disclosed data, but sponsor-funded, not
peer-reviewed in the mainstream, and demonstrated to match only the collapse’s least discriminating
feature. The live crux is narrow and testable: the A2001 girder/Column 79 connection under
realistic thermal-mechanical loading.           That is exactly what research opportunity NYC-R001
proposes (§10).

5.4 The legal aftermath: the RfC, the courts, and the data

The dispute has a full procedural trail, which the dossier records as process evidence. April 2020:
AE911Truth and ten 9/11 family members filed a Request for Correction under the Data Quality
Act, alleging eight violations in the WTC 7 report; August 2020 and June 2021: NIST denied it, initially
and finally; September 2021: suit filed in federal court; August 2022: dismissed by the D.C. District
Court; late 2023: the D.C. Circuit ruled against the plaintiffs, reasoning that the information-quality
statute does not make agencies’ reports judicially correctable for scientific accuracy; April 2024: the
Supreme Court denied certiorari, ending the case. (Architects & Engineers for 9/11 Truth) Sepa-
rately, the FOIA fight over the model data — NIST’s “might jeopardize public safety” finding and the
68,500 withheld files — was upheld in Quick v. NIST (2011), and NIST reaﬀirmed the withholding in a
2019 FAQ. (GovInfo)

Process evidence cuts both ways, and the dossier says so in both directions. For challengers: a fire-cause
conclusion that cannot be independently recomputed from released data, defended through procedural
rulings rather than replication, is a legitimate transparency grievance, and AE911Truth’s reading — that
courts held the government “not required to be scientifically accurate” — is a fair paraphrase of the
D.C. Circuit’s logic. (Architects & Engineers for 9/11 Truth) For the conventional side: the challengers’
litigation record establishes that no court was ever asked to adjudicate the engineering on the
merits — the rulings are about administrative law, not about whether Column 79 buckled — and the same
period saw NIST’s WTC 7 work summarized in the peer-reviewed Journal of Structural Engineering (2012)
and the fire-collapse mechanism joined by a real-world, independently documented instance, Tehran’s
Plasco Building (2017, §5.5). (wikipedia.org) Neither fact settles the physics; both discipline what
“suppressed” and “established” may mean in this case.

5.5 The precedent question: Plasco, and what “first known instance” proves

A recurring trope — “fire has never collapsed a steel high-rise” — was true as of 2008 in NIST’s careful
phrasing (“first known instance”), and is simply false now. On January 19, 2017, Tehran’s 17-story
steel-framed Plasco Building collapsed roughly vertically after about three hours of uncontrolled fire,
killing 20 firefighters; the event is analyzed in the peer-reviewed structural literature alongside WTC
7 as a fire-induced progressive collapse, and Wikipedia’s tally of steel high-rises lost primarily to fire
now also includes São Paulo’s Edifício Wilton Paes de Almeida and Madrid’s Windsor Tower (partial).
(wikipedia.org) Proponents correctly note the differences — Plasco was smaller, differently framed, and its
fires visibly raged through the façade — and the engineering literature itself cautions that “each building
is unique” and mechanisms cannot be transferred wholesale. (PolyU Institutional Research Archive)

The honest calibration for Erebus: the “impossibility” framing of WTC 7 (NYC-C002’s strongest a priori
objection) is dead — fire-induced total collapse of steel-framed high-rises is an observed class of event
since 2017, and NIST’s specific mechanism (thermal expansion of long-span beams walking connections
off seats at temperatures far below strength-loss thresholds) is now standard teaching in fire engineering.
(NIST) What remains genuinely open is narrower: whether this specific initiation — girder A2001 at
Column 79 under the documented fire floors — is correctly modeled, a question that survives Plasco
precisely because it is about shop-drawing-level detail, not about whether fire can fell skyscrapers. The
dossier therefore maps “first known instance” language to a context evidence record rather than letting
it carry demolition weight.

6. The Twin Towers: a strong initiation story, a never-modeled progression
6.1 What NIST established — and where its authority ends

The towers’ mechanism claim (NYC-C010) is the best-supported technical node in the whole case. The
two aircraft did catastrophic, quantified damage in under a second each — NIST’s LS-DYNA impact
simulations estimated 35 exterior columns severed in WTC 1, 33 in WTC 2, with 6 and 10 core
columns severed respectively and fireproofing stripped from trusses across 60,000–80,000 ft² — and the
buildings demonstrably withstood that (they stood 102 and 56 minutes). (Human Factors 101) The fires
did the rest: with insulation dislodged, bare steel heats to air temperature within the fire’s duration; at
1,000 °C upper-layer air temperatures (which NIST’s validated FDS fire simulations reconstructed),
unprotected steel retains roughly 10% of room-temperature strength — the metallurgy team con-
firmed the classic rule of thumb that yield strength halves by 538 °C on the recovered steel itself. (NIST)
Floor trusses sagged, pulled the perimeter columns inward (photographed bowing, tracked by NYPD
aviation in the half-hour before collapse), and the south face of WTC 1 and east face of WTC 2 buckled.
(NIST)

The popular objection — “jet fuel can’t melt steel beams” — is answered by physics nobody disputes:
nothing in the oﬀicial account requires melted steel, only weakened steel, and the melting-point meme
attacks a claim NIST never made. (Popular Mechanics) NIST’s conclusion that neither tower would
have collapsed absent dislodged fireproofing is the investigation’s single most important finding,
and it is testable in principle: it is why the 707-design-study debate (the Port Authority’s three-page
1960s talking-points document, which NIST could not verify as a rigorous analysis) is a red herring —
the 1960s study never modeled a fully-fueled, 20%-larger 767 at approach-plus speeds, nor the fire that
followed. (NIST) Initiation, in short: well supported, with the caveat that the input uncertainties were

6.2 The progression problem both sides under-model

Here is the dossier’s least comfortable sentence for the conventional side: the total, rapid, top-to-
bottom destruction of both towers has never been modeled end-to-end by anyone, including
NIST. NIST’s charter stopped at initiation; its FAQ says propagation “was readily explained without
the same complexity of modeling,” offering the static/dynamic floor-capacity calculation — an intact
floor could carry ~11 floors statically, ~6 suddenly; 12 floors (WTC 1) and 29 floors (WTC 2) came down
— as the suﬀiciency argument. (NIST) The academic carrier of the progression claim is Bažant & Zhou
(2002) and its elaborations (Bažant & Verdure 2007), a one-dimensional “crush-down/crush-up” energy
formulation concluding that once the upper block dropped a story, the kinetic energy exceeded the lower
structure’s absorption capacity by an order of magnitude — gravity alone drives unstoppable progression.
(Scientific Research Publishing)

The critics’ literature is real, if confined to low-visibility venues. MacQueen & Szamboti’s “The
Missing Jolt” (2009) argued the observed acceleration profile lacks the deceleration jolt a Bazant-
style impact should register at each floor strike; Szuladziński, Szamboti & Johns challenged Bazant’s
energy arithmetic; and a 2018 paper in Journal of Applied Mathematics and Physics (Němec et al.) derived
the free-fall-limit upper bound for the falling-mass model as g/3 — faster than the observed descent
— concluding the assumed mechanism cannot be the whole story. (Academia.edu) The engineering-
mainstream rejoinder, visible in the Metabunk technical threads, is that these critiques misread Bazant
& Zhou’s intent: it was a limit case (maximum resistance assumptions, aligned columns), not a claim of
what literally happened; the real collapse was not column-aligned piston impact but floor-slab stripping
(the “ROOSD”-style reading), for which the 11 s / 9 s “first panels down” timings are unremarkable
— NIST itself notes the cores stood 15–25 s longer and calls the total duration unknowable from the
dust-obscured video. (metabunk.org)

Grade: NYC-C011, “provisionally supported.” The collapse progression is physically plausible,
supported by order-of-magnitude energy arguments and by the absence of any measured anomaly that
requires added energy; but the decisive end-to-end model — a validated, published simulation of total
progressive collapse of a 110-story tube-frame tower — does not exist, 25 years on, and its absence is
why the towers’ “speed of fall” argument regenerates every few years. A direct, fundable fix exists (NYC-
R007): modern compute can now do what 2005 compute could not, and high-fidelity open modeling
would either retire this line permanently or find a real discrepancy.

6.3 The demolition reading of the towers fails on its own evidence

Against NYC-C003 (towers demolished), the record is not merely the absence of proof but positive
disproof of the mechanisms proposed. The collapses initiated at the impact floors — visible in every
video from multiple angles — which requires the hypothetical charges to have been placed exactly where
two unpredictably-aimed aircraft would later hit, and to have survived 56–102 minutes of fires that NIST
reconstructed at up to 1,000 °C. (NIST) The seismic record shows collapse-frequency signals beginning
~10 s after initiation and no pre-initiation spikes; the acoustic record and 1,000+ interviews contain no
blast sequence of the required intensity below the impact zones; the “puffs of dust” cited as squibs track
the piston-effect air compression NIST documents, and identical puffs were observed on fire floors before
collapse and in WTC 1 when WTC 2 was struck. (NIST)

7. The dust forensics: nanothermite, molten metal, and the deepest mystery
7.1 The red/gray chips: Harrit (2009) vs. Millette (2012)

The strongest physical-evidence claim ever advanced by the demolition side is Harrit et al., “Active
Thermitic Material Discovered in Dust from the 9/11 World Trade Center Catastrophe,”
Open Chemical Physics Journal 2:7–31 (2009) — nine authors led by Copenhagen chemist Niels Harrit,
reporting red/gray chips in four independently collected WTC dust samples whose red layer contains
~100 nm iron-oxide grains intimately mixed with plate-like aluminum, which ignite in a DSC at ~430
°C (far below conventional thermite) and produce iron-rich spheres as residue. (gammon.blog.is) The
paper’s provenance problems are, however, themselves part of the record: editor-in-chief Marie-Paule
Pileni resigned stating the paper was published without her knowledge, adding to Bentham’s existing
reputation for weak peer review; lead proponent Steven Jones had earlier been placed on paid leave by
BYU over the “increasingly speculative and accusatory nature” of his 9/11 statements; and NIST declined
to engage, citing the absence of a “clear chain of custody” for the dust — Jones’s counter-invitation for
NIST to test custody-secured dust went unanswered. (wikipedia.org)

The decisive counter-analysis arrived in 2012: MVA Scientific Consultants (James Millette), apply-
ing forensic paint-analysis standards (ASTM) plus FTIR, cross-section SEM-EDS, and TEM/SAED,
concluded the chips are consistent with carbon steel coated with epoxy resin pigmented with
iron oxide and kaolin clay — i.e., primer paint of the Tnemec type used on the towers — and found
no individual elemental aluminum particles of any size, the sine qua non of a thermitic read-
ing. (Machine Design) Harrit’s camp objected that Millette’s FTIR shows an epoxy the chips’ MEK
behavior should rule out, and no replication with agreed chain-of-custody and blind controls has ever
been run. The dossier’s grade for NYC-C014 — contradicted — reflects that the strongest independent
analysis identifies a mundane material, the supporting paper’s venue integrity collapsed, and the claim
now survives almost entirely on chain-of-custody arguments, which are procedural rather than chemical.
What would genuinely settle it is NYC-R003: blinded multi-lab analysis of custody-secured samples with
positive controls — cheap, decisive, never done.

7.2 Molten metal: the established observation, the unresolved residue

Three separable claims hide under “molten metal.” First, established: a glowing liquid poured from
WTC 2’s 80th–81st floor area in the minutes before collapse (documented in NIST NCSTAR 1-5A); NIST
attributes it to aircraft aluminum alloys (melting 475–640 °C) mixed with burning organics that shift
its color orange — a plausible and evidence-anchored reading, though the specific experiment (repro-
ducing orange-glowing aluminum-organic mixtures) has a thin literature. (NIST) Second, established
as observation: FEMA 403’s Appendix C (Barnett, Biederman & Sisson, WPI) documented severe
high-temperature sulfidation and eutectic intergranular melting on a WTC 7 beam and a tower

column — “Swiss cheese” steel, thinning one-inch sections to razor edges, at temperatures approaching
1,000 °C rather than steel’s 1,500 °C melting point — with the authors stating plainly that “no clear
explanation for the source of the sulfur has been identified,” and calling for a detailed follow-up
study that has never been performed. (wtc7.net) The New York Times called it “perhaps the deepest
mystery uncovered in the investigation.” (NIST)

Third, unresolved: persistent “molten steel in the rubble” claims. They rest on real but second-hand
testimony (cleanup workers, thermal hotspots, NASA thermal imagery of weeks-long fires) and on the
proponent literature’s interpretive leap from “hot” to “molten steel”; NIST’s inspectors and the ASCE/

nothing conclusive about pre-collapse steel condition. (NIST) The ordinary explanation — months-long
oxygen-starved fires in a heat-insulating rubble pile, plus documented eutectic corrosion that liquefies
steel surfaces well below the melting point — covers most of the testimony; the Appendix C mechanism
remains under-studied enough (sulfur source unknown; possibly gypsum wallboard, possibly acid rain
per the authors’ own speculation) that “fully explained” would be an overclaim. (wtc7.net) Erebus
structure: keep C021 and C022 established-with-known-candidates, keep C023 honestly unresolved,
and let NYC-R004 (the never-run sulfidation follow-up) carry the research flag.

7.3 What the certified dust science actually shows

The strongest dust science is not the chips debate at all — it is the exposure science. The peer-reviewed
characterization chain — Lioy et al. (2002) in Environmental Health Perspectives, the USGS studies
(Meeker et al. 2005; the 2005 Particle Atlas), and the RJ Lee Group’s 2003 “WTC Dust Signature”
report for the 130 Liberty Street damage assessment — established the dust as a caustic (pH > 11 in
places) mix dominated by pulverized concrete, gypsum, and man-made vitreous fibers, with asbestos
0.1–1.0 wt%, lead, dioxins, and PAHs, and with a reproducible “signature” (slag wool dominant) usable
for indoor-contamination forensics. (nih.gov) Iron-rich spheres — the microspherules at the center of
thermite arguments — are present in the USGS imagery and RJ Lee data, but their diagnosticity is low:
fly-ash-like spheres form whenever iron-bearing material meets high temperature and oxygen, including
in ordinary fires and in the collapse’s own friction/welding environment, which is why the proponent
literature’s “spheres = thermite” step has never carried the mainstream. (USGS Publications Warehouse)

For the case file, the dust studies’ highest-value use is neither side’s: they are the ground truth of
the health catastrophe of §2.2 and of the EPA deception of §9.1, and they demonstrate what rigorous,
custody-clean environmental forensics of this event looks like — the standard Harrit-style claims would
have to meet to be load-bearing. The dossier therefore files Lioy/USGS/RJ Lee as context evidence for
the health claims and as the methodological benchmark for any future NYC-R003 re-test.

8.2 Flight 93: what a fully excavated crash looks like

Shanksville contributes the case’s clearest baseline for “catastrophic high-speed impact into soft ground,”
which is why the “no crater, no wreckage” claims fail. The 757 hit at 563 mph, inverted, at a 40°

the bulk of the aircraft buried itself, one engine landed 2,000 ft away in a pond, light debris (paper, nylon)
scattered up to eight miles downwind at New Baltimore — a pattern standard for high-energy breakup,
not for a shootdown. (wikipedia.org) The excavation reached 40 ft until debris ran out; coroner Wally
Miller’s team recovered ~1,500 human-remains fragments (~600 lb) and identified everyone aboard
by December 21; the FDR was recovered September 13 and the CVR (buried 25 ft down) on September
14; the FBI closed the field investigation September 24, 2001. (ExploreHere)

The CVR transcript, released for the Moussaoui trial in April 2006, documents the passenger revolt; the
audio itself remains unreleased as of 2025, cited to an “active and ongoing investigation” — a residual
transparency grievance the dossier records without overweighting, since families heard the tape in a
closed 2002 session and the transcript is public. (wikipedia.org) The shootdown claims (spawned by early
debris-radius reports and the notorious Cheney “orders” confusion) collide with the radar/FDR record

any hijacked aircraft in its sights is simultaneously the strongest evidence against a shootdown and a

10. Calibration episodes: proven deception, proven error

10.2 The media anomalies: BBC’s early report and “pull it”

Two clips generate more truther traﬀic than any document, and both are resolvable to the level of what
happened, if not to everyone’s satisfaction. The BBC World report: Jane Standley announced WTC

7’s collapse at ~4:54 p.m. ET with the building visible behind her — the collapse occurred at 5:20:52
p.m. BBC editor Richard Porter’s 2007 account, reaﬀirmed in BBC’s 20th-anniversary coverage and by

“Pull it”: Silverstein’s September 2002 PBS remark — “maybe the smartest thing to do is pull it…
and they made that decision to pull and then we watched the building collapse” — was explained by
his spokesperson in 2005 as referring to withdrawing firefighters, and the FEMA report’s Chapter 5
independently records that no manual firefighting occurred inside WTC 7 (no water), i.e., there was a
withdrawal decision to refer to. (USA Today) The demolition-slang reading fails on three grounds recorded
in the dossier: “pull” is not demolition slang for explosive collapse (the counter-example cited, “pulling
Building 6,” referred to cables literally pulling wreckage); a building cannot be rigged in an afternoon; and
no insurance-fraud or other coherent motive survives the arithmetic of Silverstein’s post-attack litigation
losses. (wtc7.net) NYC-C031: contradicted. Both episodes are catalogued because Erebus’s explorer
will inevitably receive them as “new” claims from users; the tombstone-adjacent featured treatment lets
the site answer with receipts instead of dismissal.

of Jewish New Yorkers) and by its lineage as a fabricated 2001 Al-Manar/HR-net post; featuring it would
poison the case’s signal-to-noise ratio, which is precisely what tombstones exist to prevent.

13. Closing assessment
The synthesis this dossier supports, stated plainly: the core of the oﬀicial account — al-Qaeda’s
authorship, the four flights, the impact-plus-fire collapse of the towers — is as well estab-
lished as any event of its class in modern history, and the strong demolition and foreknowledge
attributions fail on the evidence their proponents themselves cite most often. The freefall of WTC 7 is
real and was real in NIST’s own final report; the put options were anomalous and were traced; the warn-
ings were red-blinking and were fragmentary. Controversy survives by fusing these into single narratives;
the decomposed record does not.

And the open residue is real, specific, and owed to the families who have done most of the forcing: a
reproducibility gap at the heart of the WTC 7 model that a court has protected rather than closed;
a sulfur-corrosion anomaly whose own discoverers’ follow-up was never run; a foreknowledge-trading
anomaly whose underlying investigative files remain sealed; an Able Danger identification dispute frozen
at “credible witnesses, destroyed record”; and a Saudi support question that a federal judge has now
sent to trial. None of these implies demolition or MIHOP; each of them is answerable with documents,
compute, or bench work that costs less than a week of the litigation they have fueled. That is the wager
of putting this case on Erebus: the contested claims of 9/11 are not a fog — they are a list,
and most of the list can be settled by work nobody has yet done.
