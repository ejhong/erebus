## 1. Scope recommendation for a first cut

9/11 is not one dispute; it is at least nine loosely-coupled dispute families
(GU's own section list: Twin Towers, WTC 7, Pentagon, the flights, military exercises,
leaders' conduct, bin Laden/hijackers, phone calls, insider trading). A first cut that
tries to cover all of them will be an encyclopedia and will fail. Both briefs
independently converge on the same tractable core, and I concur.

### Include (evidence-rich, decomposable, primary sources locatable)

1. **WTC 7 — the case's true crux** (B1 §5 calls it "the load-bearing node of the
   entire 9/11 controversy"). No aircraft, no jet fuel; NIST NCSTAR 1A mechanism vs.
   the UAF/Hulsey reevaluation; the established 2.25 s free-fall stage; the withheld
   modeling files (Quick v. NIST); the recovered-steel gap; the FEMA 403 Appendix C
   sulfidation anomaly. This cluster has the best ratio of established observations
   to genuinely contested mechanism anywhere in the case. ~7–8 featured claims.
2. **Twin Towers mechanism + the demolition/dust claims** — NIST initiation
   (well-supported), the never-modeled progression (the honest weak point of the
   conventional account), demolition-of-towers (contradicted, with positive
   disproof), Harrit nanothermite vs. Millette (contradicted), molten-metal
   observations (split into three claims per B1 §7.2). ~5–6 claims.
3. **Put options** — the cleanest observation/attribution split in the whole case:
   the anomaly is established (CBOE volumes, Poteshman, Chesney et al.), the
   interpretation is mixed (Commission trace-outs vs. non-public underlying files).
   2 claims.
4. **Foreknowledge record** — general warnings established ("the system was blinking
   red"), specific foreknowledge contradicted; FAA/NORAD inaccurate testimony
   (documented official misstatement — important negative evidence *against* the
   official narrators, not against the official account); Able Danger (genuinely
   unresolved: credible witnesses vs. destroyed records). 3–4 claims.
5. **Saudi facilitation** — the cluster where the ground has moved most since 2004:
   28 pages, EO 14040 FBI releases, Ashton v. Kingdom of Saudi Arabia trial order
   (2025-08-28, Judge Daniels). Requires the strictest living-persons handling.
   2–3 claims.
6. **Calibration episodes** — EPA "air is safe" (proven official deception, the
   anchor for calibrating institutional-trust arguments in both directions); BBC
   early report + "pull it" (established observations whose conspiratorial
   interpretations are contradicted — ideal negative-evidence exhibits). 2–3 claims.

That yields **~20–24 candidate claims** (§4 below), from which the founder can select
15–20 featured ones.

### Defer to later cuts (with reasons)

- **Pentagon and Flight 93 as full sub-cases.** The physical record is thick and the
  surviving disputes are thin (B1 §8: "strong evidence, weak controversies"). Keep one
  anchor observation claim each plus tombstones for the no-plane/shootdown families;
  full treatment adds bulk, not information.
- **Phone-calls-were-fabricated material** (GU section VIII). Popular in the
  proponent literature; contradicted by the trial record (Moussaoui exhibits) and not
  supported by either brief. Tombstone candidate, not a featured claim.
- **Military exercises (Vigilant Guardian etc.) and leaders'-conduct claims**
  (GU sections V–VI). The briefs give no primary-source spine for these beyond what
  the NORAD-testimony claim already covers.
- **Hijacker-identity / "hijackers still alive" material** (GU section VII).
  Contradicted by DNA identification-by-elimination and the FBI record (per B1/B2);
  tombstone territory.
- **KSM military-commission proceedings, detainee-torture provenance.** Handle as a
  source-provenance badge (B2's "interrogation derived / coercion concern") rather than
  as its own claim cluster in the first cut. B2: label the case "ongoing", never
  "convicted mastermind".
- **Health toll / WTC Health Program.** Context and moral framing (B1 §2.2), not a
  contested claim cluster. One context evidence record.
- **Bin Laden/al-Qaeda authorship.** Include as one anchor attribution claim
  (established) so the case is honest about its baseline, but do not spend the first
  cut re-litigating it.

### Tombstones to create at launch (per B1 §10.3, so extraction runs don't re-propose)

- No commercial airliner hit the Pentagon (missile/Global Hawk variants).
- Directed-energy weapons / mini-nukes destroyed the WTC.
- "4,000 Israelis stayed home" (antisemitic fabrication; B1 traces lineage to a 2001
  Al-Manar item).
- Flight 93 was shot down.
- The phone calls from the flights were fabricated (voice-morphing variants).

## 2. Case framing (draft text)

### whatIsClaimed (draft)

> On September 11, 2001, nineteen al-Qaeda hijackers crashed four airliners into the
> World Trade Center, the Pentagon, and a Pennsylvania field, killing 2,977 victims.
> That much is among the best-documented events in modern history. What remains
> contested is narrower and more specific than "the official story": whether NIST's
> fire-driven account of World Trade Center Building 7's collapse — the only total
> collapse of a tall steel-framed building ever attributed to fire at the time — is
> correct and reproducible; whether the total destruction of the Twin Towers required
> anything beyond impact, fire, and gravity; what the anomalous pre-attack option
> trading means; whether a U.S. military data-mining program identified the plot's
> tactical leader a year early; and how far up any Saudi-government chain the support
> given to two hijackers in California went. Around these live disputes orbits a
> larger claim — that insiders planned or permitted the attacks — which this case
> grades against the record rather than assuming or dismissing.

### whereDisagreementLives (draft)

> Almost none of the disagreement is about observations. Both sides accept the 2.25
> seconds of gravitational acceleration in WTC 7's fall (it is in NIST's own final
> report); both sides accept the put-option volume ratios; both sides accept that the
> August 6, 2001 PDB was titled "Bin Ladin Determined To Strike in US." The fight is
> at the mechanism and attribution rungs: whether fire alone can produce the WTC 7
> initiation NIST modeled (a dispute now narrowed to one girder-to-column connection
> under thermal load, argued between a partially-withheld federal model and an
> advocacy-funded open one); whether traced-to-innocuous-buyers exhausts the trading
> anomaly; whether destroyed Able Danger records covered an Atta identification;
> and whether documented assistance to two hijackers reflected Saudi institutional
> intent — a question now headed to trial. Distinct from all of these is a record of
> proven official misconduct (EPA air-safety statements edited by the White House,
> inaccurate FAA/NORAD testimony, destroyed CIA interrogation tapes) that explains —
> without vindicating — why distrust of the official account persists.

### whatWouldSettleIt (draft)

> Most of the live disputes are settleable with work nobody has done: a blinded
> independent re-analysis of the Floor 13 girder A2001 / Column 79 connection using
> both NIST's and UAF's released data; release (or bounded re-derivation) of NIST's
> ~68,500 withheld WTC 7 model files; a chain-of-custody, multi-lab re-test of the
> red/gray dust chips against thermite and Tnemec-primer controls; the never-run
> sulfur-source study FEMA 403 Appendix C's own authors called for; declassification
> of the SEC/FBI trade-tracing files behind the Commission's put-option trace-outs;
> the LIWA/DIA destruction certificates and any surviving Able Danger contractor
> data; and the Ashton v. Kingdom of Saudi Arabia trial record as it develops.

### bestConventionalExplanation (steelmanned, draft)

> Al-Qaeda, under Khalid Sheikh Mohammed's operational planning and bin Laden's
> approval, hijacked four airliners using nineteen men whose identities, travel,
> financing, and training are established by mutually independent evidence streams —
> flight and ATC records, passenger calls, DNA, immigration and financial records,
> and over 1,200 exhibits admitted in United States v. Moussaoui. The Twin Towers
> fell because the impacts severed columns and stripped fireproofing, and multi-floor
> fires weakened the remaining steel until the damaged stories gave way; NIST
> concluded neither tower would have collapsed had the fireproofing stayed on, and
> once a dozen-plus stories dropped one floor, no plausible structure could arrest
> the fall. WTC 7 fell because debris-ignited fires burned unfought for seven hours
> after the water main was cut, thermally expanding long-span floor framing until a
> girder walked off its seat at Column 79 — a mechanism requiring no steel to melt,
> since replicated as a class by Tehran's Plasco collapse (2017), and now standard
> fire-engineering teaching. The free-fall interval is the expected signature of a
> hollowed-out facade, not of demolition, which would have required jet-engine-loud
> detonations no recording captured. The pre-attack warnings were real but generic;
> the failures were bureaucratic, and the government's own inspectors general
> documented them. The put options were traced to innocuous buyers. The Saudi
> question is about the extent of facilitation by individuals, not about who flew
> the planes. Where officials misled — EPA air quality, NORAD timelines — the
> deceptions scaled to embarrassment, and were exposed by other organs of the same
> government.

## 3. Hypothesis set

Stated fairly, each with its best evidence and its best objection. These are
overlapping, not mutually exclusive — H2 is compatible with H1; H5 is compatible
with H1+H2; H3/H4 contradict parts of H1.

**H1 — Official account (Commission + NIST).** Al-Qaeda alone conceived and executed
the attacks; impact and fire destroyed WTC 1, 2, and (fire alone) 7; government
failures were of competence, not complicity. *Best evidence:* convergent independent
streams (flight data, DNA, seismic, ATC, financial records, Moussaoui exhibits);
NIST's 47-report investigation; bin Laden's 2004 claim of responsibility; KSM's
"A to Z" statement (interrogation-derived — coercion caveat). *Best objection:* its
canonical narrative leans on coerced interrogations at exactly the nodes with the
least independent corroboration (B1 §3.2: per NBC 2008 analysis, over a quarter of
Commission footnotes trace to detainee interrogations); the WTC 7 model is not
independently reproducible from released data.

**H2 — Negligence plus cover-up-of-negligence.** The attacks succeeded because of
catastrophic bureaucratic failure, and institutions then misrepresented their own
performance (NORAD/FAA timelines, EPA air statements, CIA tapes) to protect
themselves — not to conceal authorship. *Best evidence:* the government's own record:
DOJ IG on Phoenix/Moussaoui/Hazmi-Mihdhar; the Commission's tape-forced correction of
NORAD testimony; EPA IG 2003; Kean/Hamilton "set up to fail" (per brief, unverified).
*Best objection:* none serious — this hypothesis is substantially proven; the dispute
is only whether it is the whole story.

**H3 — Foreknowledge variants (LIHOP).** Some actors (U.S. insiders, foreign
services, or market participants) had specific advance knowledge and allowed the
attacks or profited. *Best evidence:* the put-option anomaly (Poteshman, Chesney et
al.); Able Danger witnesses; the destroyed/withheld records everywhere one looks.
*Best objection:* the Joint Inquiry found no intelligence identifying time, place,
and nature; Commission trace-outs of the headline trades; every documented failure
mode is dysfunction, not concealment of a known plot (B1 §9.2).

**H4 — Controlled-demolition variants (MIHOP at the buildings).** WTC 7 — and in
stronger variants WTC 1/2 — were destroyed with pre-placed devices (explosive or
incendiary/nanothermite), implying inside participation. *Best evidence:* WTC 7's
2.25 s free fall; the UAF conclusion that fire could not produce NIST's initiation;
explosion-sound testimony (Stone Woods, Bartmer, Jennings/Hess); FEMA App. C
sulfidation; Harrit et al. chips. *Best objection:* initiation at the impact floors
of unpredictably-aimed aircraft; no seismic pre-collapse spikes (Lamont-Doherty);
no 130+ dB blast signature on any recording; no demolition hardware in eight months
of debris removal (Blanchard, per brief); Millette's identification of the chips as
epoxy primer; Plasco 2017 killing the "fire has never..." impossibility argument.
B1 grades towers-demolition `contradicted`, WTC 7-demolition `mixed` — the honest gap
between those two grades is the case's most delicate editorial decision.

**H5 — Saudi-facilitation variants.** Individuals connected to Saudi government
institutions (al-Bayoumi, al-Thumairy) knowingly supported Hazmi and Mihdhar; in
stronger variants that support was institutionally directed. *Best evidence:* the 28
pages; EO 14040 FBI releases (plaintiffs characterize one as identifying al-Bayoumi
as a Saudi General Intelligence Presidency agent); the 2025 Daniels ruling sending
the case to trial. *Best objection:* the Commission/White House formulation — no
evidence the Saudi government "as an institution or senior Saudi officials
individually" funded al-Qaeda (per brief, unverified) — and the fact that the 2025
ruling is jurisdictional, not a merits verdict. B2 insists on a four-way proposition
split (assistance documented → relationships documented → knowing facilitation
disputed → state sponsorship not established), which §4 adopts.

## 4. Candidate claims (24)

Format: **ID | proposed frozen wording | theme | rung | suggested credibility |
suggested diagnosticity | sources bearing on it** (source IDs point into §5).
Credibility states from the case vocabulary; diagnosticity is how much the claim,
if true, discriminates among H1–H5. All wordings are drafts for freezing per

### WTC 7 cluster

**C01.** WTC 7, a 47-story steel-framed building not struck by an aircraft, collapsed
completely at about 5:20 p.m. on September 11, 2001, after uncontrolled fires on
multiple floors burned for hours with no water supply to fight them.
— wtc7 | observation | `established` | LOW (all hypotheses accept it) | S-N1, S-N2, S-C1.

**C02.** During its collapse, WTC 7's north face descended at gravitational
acceleration (free fall) for approximately 2.25 seconds over approximately 32 m
(~8 stories), as stated in NIST's final report after re-analysis prompted by public
comment.
— wtc7 | observation | `established` | MODERATE (bounded: B1 §5.2 — free fall is a
necessary feature of any plausible account including NIST's, so it discriminates
less than either side advertises) | S-N2, S-N3, S-A1, S-BM.

**C03.** NIST's probable collapse sequence for WTC 7 — thermal expansion walking the
Floor 13 girder (A2001) off its seat at Column 79, cascading floor failures, and
buckling of Column 79 — occurred with modeled column temperatures below ~300 °C,
relying on thermal expansion rather than steel strength loss.
— wtc7 | observation-about-the-record | `established` (as a statement of what NCSTAR
1A says) | LOW alone; frames C04 | S-N1, S-N2.

**C04.** Fire-induced thermal expansion initiated WTC 7's collapse via the girder
A2001 / Column 79 walk-off sequence described in NIST NCSTAR 1A.
— wtc7 | mechanism | `mixed` (B1 §5.3: internally consistent, best-resourced, never
independently reproduced, resting on withheld model files; B2: "best-supported
published official explanation"; editors flagged to consider `well_supported`) |
HIGH | S-N1, S-N2, S-N4, S-U1, S-M1, S-M2, S-P1 (Plasco, supporting the mechanism
class), S-J1 (JSE 2012 summary, per brief).

**C05.** The University of Alaska Fairbanks reevaluation (Hulsey et al., final report
March 25, 2020) correctly concluded that the observed WTC 7 collapse can be matched
only by near-simultaneous failure of every column over ~8 stories.
— wtc7 | mechanism | `weakly_supported` (B1: serious modeling effort with a released
256 GB dataset, but sponsor-funded ($316,153 from AE911Truth), reviewed by
sponsor-sympathetic academics (e.g., Korol), not mainstream peer-reviewed, and its
best-fit simulation reproduces only free fall while failing the roofline kink,
penthouse timing, and rotational deformation per Metabunk critiques) | MODERATE |
S-U1, S-U2, S-M2, S-M3, S-C2, S-A1.

**C06.** NIST withheld approximately 68,500 of ~91,114 responsive WTC 7 modeling
data files under a July 2009 NCST Act §7(d) finding that release "might jeopardize
public safety," a withholding upheld by the U.S. District Court for D.C. in Quick v.
NIST (April 7, 2011); the initiating mechanism therefore cannot be fully reproduced
by independent engineers from NIST's published materials.
— wtc7 / transparency | observation (process) | `established` | MODERATE (a
transparency fact, not a physics fact — B1: process evidence "cuts both ways") |
S-G1, S-A2, S-Q1.

**C07.** No identified structural steel from WTC 7 was recovered for forensic
examination; unmarked pieces were recycled before cataloguing, unlike the color-coded
tower steel.
— wtc7 | observation | `established` (NIST states it openly, per B1 §3.3) | MODERATE
(B1: "the single largest physical-evidence gap in the whole case") | S-N2, S-N1.

**C08.** FEMA 403 Appendix C (Barnett, Biederman & Sisson, WPI) documented severe
high-temperature sulfidation and eutectic intergranular melting on one WTC 7 beam
and one tower column, with the authors stating that no clear explanation for the
sulfur source had been identified and recommending a follow-up study that has never
been performed.
— dust/steel | observation | `established` | MODERATE (a real, uncontested anomaly
with mundane candidate explanations — gypsum, acid rain per the authors — never
tested) | S-F1, S-F2, S-BM.

**C09.** Witnesses at WTC 7 — including NY1 correspondent Gigi Stone Woods, NYPD
officer Craig Bartmer, and building occupants Barry Jennings and Michael Hess —
reported loud explosion-like sounds associated with the building on 9/11, and NIST's
statement that no witness reports of blast sounds existed is contradicted by this
testimony.
— wtc7 | observation | `established` (that the testimony exists; B1 §5.6) | LOW-to-
MODERATE | S-A3, S-N2, S-BM (Appendix A), S-GU.
*Split-companion:* the Jennings/Hess timeline discrepancy (explosion account at the
6th-floor stair landing vs. NIST's timeline; Hess later aligned with NIST, Jennings
did not) deserves one honest evidence row, per B1: "never satisfactorily reconciled."

**C10.** The explosion-like sounds reported around WTC 7 were demolition charges.
— wtc7 | mechanism | `contradicted` (B1 §5.6: NIST's acoustic threshold — the
smallest column-severing charge would produce 130–140 dB at half a mile — appears on
no recording; none of the testimony describes a shock-front signature; collapsing
structure/transformers/debris are physically adequate for loud booms) | HIGH if
true | S-N2, S-A3.

**C11.** WTC 7 was deliberately demolished.
— wtc7 | attribution | `mixed`-to-`weakly_supported` (B1 grades its C002 `mixed`;
note the deliberate asymmetry with C19 below. B2 grades demolition "not supported as
well as fire-induced failure." Recommend the case display this claim as the
dependent top rung of C04/C05, with `depends_on` links) | HIGH | all WTC 7 sources.

### Twin Towers cluster

**C15.** The red/gray chips found in WTC dust samples (Harrit et al. 2009) are

**C16.** A glowing molten liquid poured from the 80th–81st-floor area of WTC 2's
northeast corner in the minutes before its collapse.
— dust/steel | observation | `established` (documented in NIST NCSTAR 1-5A, per B1)
| MODERATE (NIST's aluminum-plus-organics reading is plausible and evidence-anchored
but has a thin experimental literature, per B1 §7.2) | S-N5, S-N6.

**C17.** Molten steel persisted for weeks in the WTC rubble pile.
— dust/steel | observation | `unresolved` (B1: real but second-hand testimony plus
thermal imagery of hot spots; NIST/ASCE steel surveys found no melted steel;
"hot" ≠ "molten steel"; eutectic surface liquefaction and oxygen-starved rubble

### Trading cluster

— trading | observation | `established` (the volumes and the published findings are
not in dispute; B1 §9.1) | HIGH as an anomaly; the discrimination lives in C19 |
S-PO1, S-CH1, S-SN1, S-CR1.

**C19.** The anomalous pre-9/11 option trading reflected advance knowledge of the
attacks.
— trading | attribution | `mixed` (B1: the Commission traced 95% of the Sept 6 UAL
puts to a single U.S. institutional investor "with no conceivable ties to al Qaeda"
and the AMR spike to a newsletter recommendation, and reported that "the apparently
suspicious consistently proved innocuous" (quotes per brief, unverified); but the
underlying SEC/FBI trade-tracing files remain non-public, so the trace-outs cannot
be independently re-verified, and the academic pattern findings survive in aggregate.
B1 §12.3 flags `weakly_supported` as defensible) | HIGH | S-CR1, S-PO1, S-CH1, S-GU
(as claim-origin: GU's three objections — assertion, circularity, econometrics).

## 7. Traps and cautions

### The briefs themselves

- **Both briefs are AI deep-research products** (B1 is stamped "Generated by Kimi"
  on nearly every page). B1's self-assigned "ai_verified" labels are *its* claims,
  not ours; treat every citation as unverified until checked. B1 references a
  companion drop-in folder (claims.yaml etc.) that **does not exist in this repo** —
  do not go looking for it or trust its claimed record counts (26 claims, 58
  evidence records, 46 sources).
- **B1 leans heavily on Wikipedia and secondary aggregators** for load-bearing facts
  (its citation keys are literally "(wikipedia.org)", "(Human Factors 101)",
  "(Snopes.com)"). Its NCSTAR 1 link is a third-party mirror on a human-factors
  blog. Every such fact needs re-anchoring to the primary document.
- **Several URLs in B1 are visibly truncated or contain ligature artifacts** from
  the PDF-to-text rendering (flagged inline in §5). Do not paste them into records
  as-is.
- **The two briefs disagree on the canonical impact times:** B1 uses NIST
  video-derived times (8:46:30, 9:02:59, 9:37:45) with Lamont-Doherty seismic values
  (8:46:26, 9:02:54); B2 uses Commission times (8:46:40, 9:03:11, 9:37:46). Neither
  is wrong; they are different measurement chains. The case must pick one anchor per
  claim and cite the chain, or the timeline will look self-contradictory.
- **B1 editorializes** (e.g., "the refusal of every incumbent institution to run
  them for 25 years is itself a data point"). Its grades are drafts to be argued,
  not findings — it flags three of its own judgment calls for human review (C012
  mixed-vs-well_supported; C025 mixed-vs-weakly_supported; tombstone language),
  and this digest inherits those flags.

- **No-plane-at-the-Pentagon** — contradicted by FDR, 184 identified victims (five

  edited (AP, per brief).

  tapes-based finding that the military never had any hijacked aircraft in its

  rejection reasons.

  not melting).

  ch. 1 prior on the pre-Plasco record; GU likewise. Any use of the impossibility
  framing must carry the update.
- **Misquoted/misused first-responder testimony** — BM Appendix A and GU aggregate
  explosion accounts; B1's assessment stands: the observation (people heard loud
  booms) is established, the inference (demolition charges) is contradicted by the

  work. The Jennings/Hess episode is the one honest residual.

### Independence problems (AGENTS.md §3.10)

- **The proponent literature is a tight citation loop:** the GU panel includes
  Harrit, Jones, Chandler, Szamboti, MacQueen; BM's review committee includes Korol
  and MacQueen; Korol also peer-reviewed the UAF report; AE911Truth funded UAF and
  published the witness compilations; GU cites Harrit; BM cites Harrit and Korol.
  For independence-group purposes, **AE911Truth / GU panel / BM / UAF review / the
  Journal of 9/11 Studies constitute ONE evidence stream**, not five.
- **The conventional side has its own dependency knots:** NIST's WTC 7 conclusions
  cannot be independently recomputed (withheld files); the Commission's plot
  narrative depends at key nodes on coerced interrogations (per NBC analysis via
  B1, over a quarter of footnotes; Zelikow's concession), and the CIA destroyed the
  tapes. "The Commission says X citing a KSM interrogation" and "KSM said X" are one
  source (B2's exact example). Wire-copy duplication (e.g., the Reuters WTC 7 error
  propagating to BBC/CNN) is one source.
- **Both chemistry keystones are outside normal peer review:** Harrit et al.
  (Bentham venue collapse) AND Millette/MVA (never journal-published). Label both
  accordingly; neither gets authority the other is denied on venue grounds alone.
- **B2's badge taxonomy** (interrogation-derived / coercion concern / litigation
  allegation / advocacy funded / model dependent / not independently replicated /
  judicial finding vs. filing) should be adopted wholesale into the case's evidence
  records.
