## 2. [study] Frozen-criteria table of documented fire-induced collapses in steel-framed high-rise buildings

**Question / truth condition:** Across the documented world record of steel-framed buildings above roughly 12 storeys subjected to uncontrolled multi-floor fire without effective suppression, how many experienced total progressive collapse, how many partial collapse, and how many none — and for each case, what are the frozen comparison fields: height, framing system (tube-frame, moment-frame, braced core), presence/condition of fireproofing, water availability, fire duration, and collapse outcome? **What it would settle:** If the table shows multiple qualifying total collapses once no-suppression and compromised-fireproofing conditions are matched, the 'unprecedented, therefore suspect' inference behind C010 loses its comparative footing.
