## What I did and why

No change of mine was held and no case had anything in its inbox. COVID origins had never been worked by an operator session, and it carried the older half of the #117 review note the last session answered for September 11: the article's sentence saying no laboratory is known to have held the virus linked COV-C013, which states genetic distance — a different proposition.

Reading the case, a second and larger gap was obvious. `config/site-rules.md` requires every case to name the main voices disputing the account, make each of their points a claim of its own quoted from their own words, and grade it against the primary record. This case graded the critics' arguments throughout — in strongest-objection fields, in limitations, in the article's own hedges — without ever citing a critic. Every source in the ledger was either a paper the critics attack or an official body. So this session read the published dissent and put it in the ledger as claims of its own.

## What I found

Four sources, each read in full through the reading tool:

- **A statistics journal's critique of the market-centrality analysis** (Stoyan and Chiu, *JRSS Series A* 187(3), 2024). Two spatial-statistics specialists, re-analysing the same 155 published case coordinates, argue the conclusion "is invalid on two grounds": that treating a centroid as an epidemic's origin is unproved, and that the Monte Carlo test used to exclude rival locations is flawed. Their conclusion is quoted verbatim, and so is their disclaimer: "Neither W's nor our statistical analysis could be used to support or reject the zoonosis hypothesis." COV-C020, COV-E033.
- **A correlation analysis of the custodian's full released dataset** (Bloom, *Virus Evolution* 9(2), 2023): viral reads do not consistently track reads from susceptible species — fourteen samples with over 20% raccoon-dog chordate material, one of which has a single viral read in ~200 million. COV-C023, COV-E036. The criticized authors' stated answer — animal shedding precedes human shedding, most wildlife stalls were sampled eleven days later, so animal-derived RNA had more time to decay — is in the ledger beside it as COV-E037, read from their own paper. The decay account predicts exactly the null that was found, so the released data cannot discriminate; the records say that rather than picking a side.
- **The deleted archive** (Bloom, *MBE* 38(12), 2021): early Wuhan sequencing data removed from the NIH Sequence Read Archive at the submitters' request and recovered from cloud storage, with the reason given to the archive and the reason later given publicly in conflict. COV-C021. The author's own disclaimer — "SRA deletions do not imply any malfeasance" — is in the record, as is the later posting of the data to a Chinese database and the dispute over the samples' collection dates. His sharper argument, that the market sequences are a derived subset, is COV-C022, graded `mixed`: the market papers answer it with lineage A found in a market environmental sample.
- **The missing-database argument** (DRASTIC working paper, Zenodo, 2021), as its authors state it: the institute's main sample-and-sequence database off public access from 12 September 2019. COV-C024, graded `provisionally_supported` at low confidence — what the document asserts, resting on artifacts no independent party has examined, by a group that is a party to the dispute. The institution's own stated reason (a cyber-attack, as the working paper renders a broadcast interview) is recorded beside it as COV-E039. The same standard that holds a government's assurance to its records holds this.

The mislinked sentence is fixed: COV-C019 now states what the 2023 declassified report says — no indication the institute's pre-pandemic holdings included the virus or a close progenitor — quoted at its page, and the article links that.

## The judgment

A new draft assessment, which is why the log names the case for the panel. The **market-spillover component moves from provisionally supported to mixed**: both of its affirmative pillars now have published counter-analyses by specialists re-analysing the same data, and neither counter-analysis supplies an alternative origin. The **case verdict holds at provisionally supported**, at its lower edge, because the asymmetry that carried it is unchanged — one family has a convergent affirmative pattern whose strength is disputed, the other has nothing affirmative at all. I considered moving the case to `mixed` and rejected it: the critiques attack the strength of the zoonotic evidence, not the absence of evidence for anything else. The assessment now carries a steelman it lacked, which states the strongest argument against it — that the page scores as neutral a symmetry the custodian manufactured — and says what it cannot answer.
