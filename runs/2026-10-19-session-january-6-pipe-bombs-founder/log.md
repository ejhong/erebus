# Open the J6 pipe-bomb case

A founder session (Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code, working with the founder),
not a scheduled operator session. The founder asked for the case on
2026-10-19: "a place where somebody can come and get a true picture of what is
going on", pulling in as much as can be pulled in; "address every one of the
points steve baker raised … into claims"; "assess the rumble video of cole
walking around somewhere else that night"; and, as the case was being
written, "look at bakers latest posts", whose congressional footage and
timestamps "are key".

## What was done

- **Read** the public court record of United States v. Cole (D.D.C.
  No. 1:26-cr-00001-AHA) through the engine's reading tool: the complaint
  affidavit; the government's detention memorandum and the defense's
  opposition; the January 28, 2026 hearing transcript; the joint status report
  on the defense's subpoenas; the defense's false-confession expert report and
  its notice that the interview recording is held by counsel; the government's
  motion on the post-arrest diagnosis; the motions on the third superseding
  indictment. Also the House subcommittees' interim report of January 2025
  (from the Internet Archive; the committee's link is dead), CNN, the AP,
  NBC, The National News Desk, HuffPost and the Washington Examiner, and
  Steve Baker's posts on X and his two October 2026 interviews on Watching the
  Watchers.
- **Wrote** the ledger: 72 claims, 153 evidence records (every quoted passage
  found in its source by the quote check), 31 sources, eight decisive tests,
  and a pre-registered study (PIPE-S001) of whether the phone's cell sectors
  reach the places in the October footage, frozen with no rows.
- **Baker's points**, each a claim of its own, attributed and graded: the
  October 18 footage (north side of the Capitol; mask, jacket, shoes; family
  recognition), and from October 19 its source (the House Administration
  Subcommittee on Oversight's Rumble release), cameras 602 and 940, and
  20:03:32 to 20:04:02; shoe size; the podiatrist; the smartwatch; no video of
  the RNC placement; not overnight; the flame test; the missing end cap; the
  second disruption shot; the timer at twenty minutes; the January 6 DNC
  footage; the 95 denials; the insider.
- **Judged** (draft, unratified): the charged account well supported on the
  record as sworn and proffered, untested at trial. The decisive point the
  footage raises: the affidavit puts the bomber at New Jersey Avenue and E
  Street SE at 7:59:38; Baker's man is on Constitution Avenue NW at 20:03:32,
  about a kilometre away. If both clocks are right, one identification is
  wrong; so the clocks and the man's identity decide it.
- **Stamped** the case's first edition with the engine's new opening path.

## Later the same day: Baker's latest posts, and a correction

At the founder's direction ("look at bakers latest posts"), the newest posts
were read in full: Baker says the footage is "uncut and unaltered" on the
House Administration Subcommittee on Oversight's Rumble channel, with the
camera numbers in the video for anyone to check; Armitas, who made the video,
says it begins at 7:57 p.m. and that the phone data stand but do not exclude
the north side; a critic says the times on screen were added by Baker's team.
Each is now a claim of its own. The engine's reader now takes a long X post's
whole text from a mirror of X's data when X's embed cuts it short (upstream
1c4100c), so these could be quoted.

Correction before publication: the first stamp said Cole's records showed
every kind of part in the devices. The affidavit itemizes six, and the only
alligator clips it lists were bought on January 23, 2021, after the
placements, as Baker had pointed out. The purchase claim, the article and the
judgment now say so, and the point is a claim of its own. The case was
re-stamped (the earlier stamp was never published). The article and records
also no longer list the powder's ingredients: what the case needs is that the
confession names a brand and a store, which can be checked.

## The living-persons rules, as applied

Allegations are attributed (the affidavit, the prosecution, the defense,
Baker). No uncharged person is named: the former Capitol Police officer whom a
retracted November 2025 report accused is described without her name, and the
court filing that names the defense's subpoena recipients is quoted only where
it does not. Cole's relatives and a witness named in Baker's material are not
named.
